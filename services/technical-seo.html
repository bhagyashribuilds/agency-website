Technical SEO for Websites That Deserve to Rank

Engineer-led, not marketer-led. If your site is genuinely good and still invisible, the cause is usually technical and specific — a crawl rule, a rendering problem, a slow page, a missing canonical. I find those and fix them myself, rather than handing you a PDF and wishing you luck.

What an engagement covers

  • Crawlability and indexing — robots.txt, sitemaps, canonical tags, stray noindex directives, redirect chains, and duplicate URLs. This is where most "why aren't we showing up" problems actually live.
  • Core Web Vitals — LCP, INP, and CLS measured properly, then the specific causes fixed: oversized images, render-blocking resources, layout shift from missing dimensions, lazy-loading applied where it hurts.
  • Structured data — JSON-LD schema that reflects what's genuinely on the page, which increasingly determines how AI-powered search and assistants describe you.
  • Site architecture and internal linking — making sure your important pages are reachable and that link value flows toward the ones meant to rank.
  • On-page fundamentals — unique titles and meta descriptions, sane heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text.
  • JavaScript rendering — whether crawlers actually see your content, which is a common and invisible failure on app-driven front-ends.
  • Mobile experience — indexing is mobile-first, so this isn't optional.

What the audit deliverable looks like

Not a 60-page automated export. A findings document with:

  • Each issue stated plainly, with the evidence for it.
  • Why it matters — what it's actually costing you, or honestly, whether it's marginal.
  • A priority: fix now, fix soon, or leave it.
  • An effort estimate, so you can decide what to hand back to me and what to do yourself.

Anything I can't verify gets labelled as unverified rather than presented as fact. You should expect that from anyone doing this work.

What I won't do

No purchased links, private blog networks, cloaking, doorway pages, hidden text, spun content, or fake reviews. Those carry real risk of a manual penalty, and they're not a service I offer at any price. I also won't build near-duplicate location pages for every neighbourhood — it doesn't work, and it makes a site look manipulative.

Related: if your site is dated as well as slow, website redesign covers both, and rankings are preserved through the migration. If crawlers can't see content because of how the front-end renders, that's backend and API territory too.
