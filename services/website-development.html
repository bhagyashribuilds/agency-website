Website Development That's Fast, SEO-Friendly, and Built to Last

I build business websites and landing pages as a freelance full-stack developer in Mumbai, working remotely with clients across India. Seven-plus years of production experience, performance treated as a requirement rather than a nice-to-have, and full code ownership on handover.

What's included

  • Responsive build: one codebase that holds up on phones, tablets, and desktop, checked on real viewport sizes rather than assumed.
  • Performance work as part of the build: image sizing, lazy-loading below the fold, sensible font loading, and no framework weight the site doesn't need.
  • On-page SEO foundations: unique title and meta description per page, canonical tags, Open Graph and Twitter card tags, clean semantic headings, and a sitemap.
  • Accessible markup: real labels on form fields, keyboard-reachable navigation, and sufficient colour contrast.
  • Hosting, DNS, and SSL setup: either on a platform I set up for you, or into hosting you already have.
  • Cross-browser and mobile QA before anything reaches you.
  • Full handover: source code, assets, and credentials. You own all of it.

Who this is for

This service fits three groups in particular:

  • Small businesses that need a credible, fast site which actually gets found, not a template that loads in six seconds.
  • Founders who need a marketing site alongside a product, and want the same person to be able to handle both.
  • Agencies needing overflow capacity from a developer who can work to a spec without hand-holding.

If what you actually need is an application with user accounts, dashboards, or business logic rather than pages, web application development is the closer fit. If the site already exists and the problem is that it's dated or slow, look at website redesign and modernization instead.
